
# 13 - Target Company Watch List
## Executive Operating Manual
### Evan Forrester Parnell

> "A target company list is not a list of employers. It is a prioritized map of organizations whose business problems align with the Executive's strengths."

---

# Purpose

This chapter establishes a living watch list methodology rather than a static directory. The objective is to focus attention on organizations where organizational engineering, operational transformation, and scalable leadership create disproportionate value.

---

# Selection Criteria

Target organizations generally exhibit most of the following:

- Founder-led or founder influenced
- Private equity-backed or likely acquisition candidates
- $20M–$300M annual revenue (flexible)
- Multi-site operations
- Technical or industrial business model
- Operational complexity increasing rapidly
- Growth through acquisition or geographic expansion
- Need for professional management systems

---

# Tier 1 – Highest Priority

## Private Equity Portfolio Companies
**Why:** Highest alignment with transformation, scaling, and value creation.

Monitor:
- New acquisitions
- CEO / COO changes
- Integration announcements
- Growth capital investments

---

## Industrial & Technical Services

Examples include:
- HVAC and mechanical services
- Environmental services
- Water and wastewater
- Industrial cleaning
- Equipment dealerships
- Specialty contracting
- Laboratory and testing services
- Infrastructure maintenance

---

## Buy-and-Build Platforms

Characteristics:

- Multiple acquisitions annually
- Decentralized operations
- Need for integration
- Standardization initiatives
- Executive hiring after acquisitions

---

# Tier 2 – Strong Opportunities

- Business services
- Engineering support firms
- Building automation
- Mission-critical facilities
- Industrial distribution
- Technical field services

---

# Tier 3 – Opportunistic

Pursue selectively when the mandate clearly emphasizes:

- organizational transformation
- operational excellence
- leadership development
- systems implementation
- scaling

Avoid pursuing solely because of compensation or title.

---

# Active Monitoring Categories

Review weekly:

- Company career pages
- LinkedIn executive postings
- Private equity acquisition news
- Executive recruiter postings
- Industry association job boards

Review monthly:

- Press releases
- Leadership changes
- Expansion announcements
- New funding rounds

---

# Living Watch List Structure

Maintain the following fields for each company:

- Company
- Industry
- Revenue (estimate)
- Ownership
- Headquarters
- Geographic fit
- Target role(s)
- Key contacts
- Recruiters
- Recent news
- Current openings
- Next follow-up date
- Notes

# Closing Reflection

A watch list is valuable only if maintained.

The objective is not to remember companies.

The objective is to recognize opportunity before the broader market does.

---

# Key Takeaways

1. Build and maintain a living target list.
2. Monitor ownership changes as closely as job postings.
3. Evaluate companies against business problems, not titles.
4. Use a structured scorecard to prioritize effort.
5. Preserve future framework ideas in a separate editorial companion document.
