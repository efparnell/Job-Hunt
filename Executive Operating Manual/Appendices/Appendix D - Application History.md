
# Appendix D - Application History
## Executive Operating Manual
### Evan Forrester Parnell

> "An application history is not a log of jobs pursued. It is a dataset describing how the market responds to executive positioning."

---

# Purpose

Maintain a complete historical record of every meaningful opportunity pursued.

Over time this appendix becomes a strategic dataset that informs resume evolution, networking priorities, target industries, compensation expectations, and interview preparation.

---

# Record Structure

For each opportunity record:

- Date
- Company
- Role
- Industry
- Location
- Ownership (Founder, PE, Public, Family)
- Compensation Range
- Resume Version Used
- Cover Letter Used
- Recruiter / Hiring Manager
- Networking Actions
- Interview Stages
- Final Outcome
- Rejection Reason (if known)
- Lessons Learned
- Follow-up Date

---

# Outcome Codes

- Applied
- Acknowledged
- Recruiter Screen
- Hiring Manager Interview
- Executive Interview
- Finalist
- Offer
- Accepted
- Declined
- Rejected
- Withdrawn
- Future Target

---

# Metrics Dashboard

Track annually:

- Applications
- Interviews
- Interview rate
- Offer rate
- Average compensation
- Median response time
- Best-performing resume
- Best-performing industries
- Strongest networking sources

Measure quality, not volume.

---

# Reflection Template

After every completed opportunity answer:

1. Why was this a fit?
2. What business problem were they solving?
3. What resonated?
4. What did not?
5. What should change before the next application?
