
# Appendix C - Company Reviews
## Executive Operating Manual

> "Every application teaches the market something about the Executive. Every company teaches the Executive something about the market."

---

# Purpose

Maintain structured reviews of organizations evaluated during the executive search.

The objective is not simply to remember companies, but to improve decision quality through pattern recognition.

---

# Review Template

For each company capture:

- Company
- Industry
- Ownership (Founder, PE, Public, Family)
- Estimated Revenue
- Geography
- Target Role
- Compensation
- Interview Stage
- Final Outcome

## Strategic Evaluation

Rate 1–5:

- Mission fit
- Leadership scope
- Operational complexity
- Transformation opportunity
- Private equity relevance
- Learning potential
- Long-term career value

## Notes

Document:

- Leadership impressions
- Cultural observations
- Interview experience
- Recruiter quality
- Business problems discussed
- Lessons learned

---

# Outcome Categories

- Applied
- Screened
- Interviewed
- Finalist
- Offer
- Declined
- Rejected
- Withdrew
- Future Target

---

# Pattern Recognition

Review quarterly:

- Which industries respond?
- Which resume versions perform best?
- Common rejection reasons.
- Compensation trends.
- Geographic trends.
- Recruiter relationships.

---

# Long-Term Value

Over time this appendix becomes proprietary market intelligence that improves future search strategy and consulting positioning.
