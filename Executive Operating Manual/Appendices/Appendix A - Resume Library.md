
# Appendix A - Resume Library
## Executive Operating Manual
### Evan Forrester Parnell

> "A resume library is institutional memory for an executive career."

---

# Purpose

This appendix defines the structure, governance, and long-term maintenance of the Executive's resume portfolio.

Rather than maintaining a single resume, maintain a curated library of strategically positioned resumes that can be rapidly tailored without rewriting from scratch.

---

# Resume Portfolio

## Executive Core Resume (Master)
The authoritative source containing every significant accomplishment, metric, certification, leadership role, project, and career milestone.

**Never apply with this version.**
It is the source from which all others are derived.

---

## Production Resumes

Maintain current versions for:

- COO / Chief Operating Officer
- General Manager
- VP / Director of Operations
- Private Equity Portfolio Executive
- Operating Partner (future)
- Fractional COO / Consultant
- Board Candidate
- Speaker / Author (future)

Each should emphasize different evidence while preserving one consistent executive narrative.

---

# Supporting Libraries

Each resume should draw from centralized libraries instead of reinventing content.

## Achievement Library
Maintain quantified accomplishments with supporting evidence.

Fields:
- Business problem
- Action
- Result
- Metrics
- Date
- Supporting documentation

## Executive Story Library
Maintain interview-ready stories organized by:
- Leadership
- Transformation
- Growth
- Crisis
- Change management
- Financial performance
- Culture
- Failure and lessons learned

## Skills Library
Categorize by:
- Operational leadership
- Organizational engineering
- Finance
- Strategy
- AI & automation
- Continuous improvement
- Governance
- Technology

---

# Version Control

Every resume should record:

- Version number
- Date updated
- Target audience
- Major revisions
- Market feedback incorporated

Archive superseded versions rather than overwriting history.

---

# Resume Development Workflow

Career Event
    ↓
Capture accomplishment
    ↓
Update Achievement Library
    ↓
Update Story Library
    ↓
Revise Master Resume
    ↓
Refresh Production Resumes

Knowledge should flow once and be reused everywhere.

---

# Constitution vs. Prompt (Editorial Note)

A significant realization during development of this manual was that future AI systems should not be driven solely by prompts.

Prompts explain **what to do**.

A constitution explains **how to think**.

The long-term vision is to maintain an AI Constitution that preserves enduring principles—systems thinking, diagnosis before prescription, knowledge preservation, and continuous improvement—while prompts become disposable task-specific instructions.

This distinction should become a foundational concept in the future Executive Intelligence System and Organizational Intelligence Engineering methodology.
