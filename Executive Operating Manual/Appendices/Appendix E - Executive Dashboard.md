
# Appendix E - Executive Dashboard
## Executive Operating Manual
### Evan Forrester Parnell

> "What gets measured gets managed. What gets visualized gets understood."

---

# Purpose

The Executive Dashboard is the operational command center for managing an executive career as an ongoing system rather than a series of disconnected activities.

It should consolidate the most important indicators into a single view that supports timely, informed decisions.

---

# Executive Scorecard

## Career Pipeline

Track:

- Target Companies
- Active Applications
- Recruiter Conversations
- Networking Conversations
- Interviews Scheduled
- Offers
- Active Consulting Leads

Review weekly.

---

## Opportunity Quality

Monitor:

- Average Strategic Fit Score
- Average Compensation
- Geographic Alignment
- Private Equity Alignment
- Transformation Opportunity
- Leadership Scope

Quality is more important than quantity.

---

## Networking Health

Metrics:

- New Executive Connections (30 days)
- Follow-ups Due
- Recruiter Touchpoints
- Operating Partner Relationships
- Founder / CEO Relationships
- Dormant High-Value Contacts

---

## Learning Dashboard

Track:

- New Lessons Captured
- Stories Added
- Resume Updates
- Frameworks Created
- Editorial Notes Logged
- Books / Articles Reviewed

Institutional knowledge should compound continuously.

---

## Consulting Development

Track progress for:

- Organizational Intelligence Engineering
- AI Governance Framework
- Executive Operating Manual
- Case Study Library
- Speaking Opportunities
- Service Offerings
- Future Software Concepts

---

## AI Governance Dashboard

Measure:

- Constitutions Created
- Projects Using Constitutions
- Prompt Libraries
- Knowledge Bases
- AI Workflow Playbooks
- Automation Coverage

The objective is governed intelligence—not automation for its own sake.

---

## Quarterly Executive Review

Every quarter answer:

1. What created the greatest value?
2. What should stop?
3. What should scale?
4. What capabilities were developed?
5. What relationships strengthened?
6. What new opportunities emerged?

---

## Annual Executive Review

Review:

- Career trajectory
- Leadership growth
- Compensation progression
- Network quality
- Consulting maturity
- Intellectual property created
- Organizational impact

Update long-term goals accordingly.

# Dashboard Architecture

Executive Vision
    ↓
Objectives
    ↓
Key Results
    ↓
Metrics
    ↓
Projects
    ↓
Actions
    ↓
Reflection

Every metric should support a decision.
