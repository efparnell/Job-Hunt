# Retained-search outreach — Stuart Glassman (Stanton Chase) and Ash Kolady (JM Search)
Drafted 9/29/26. These two matter because the pipeline's one interview came through a recruiter and nothing else did. Unlike a job posting, these don't expire — but they also don't pay off in a week. Send them and forget them.

## The rule that makes recruiter outreach work

A recruiter's inbox is full of candidates asking to be placed. Almost nobody sends them useful information. So neither note asks for a job. Both offer something — market observation from an operator inside the sector — and ask one small, specific question. That gets replies. "Are you working on anything for someone like me?" does not.

Second rule: be explicit about the band and the geography in the first few lines. Recruiters sort by fit to live mandates. Making them guess is how you get filed and forgotten.

## Follow-up discipline

- Log both sends in the tracker as Networking rows, or as one row each, with a next action 14 days out.
- One follow-up only if silent, two weeks later, adding a new piece of information rather than repeating the ask.
- If either replies, the goal of the first conversation is **not** to be submitted for something. It's to be memorable and to learn what they're seeing. Ask what their clients are struggling to hire for.
- Neither of these is a substitute for the WillScot and Microbac applications. Applications are this week; this channel pays off next quarter.
