
# Editorial Companion Notes (Seed)

## New Foundational Distinction

### Prompt vs. Constitution

One of the most important conceptual breakthroughs of the project:

- A **prompt** tells an AI what to do.
- A **constitution** tells an AI how to think.

Prompts are tactical and disposable.
Constitutions are strategic and durable.

Future Executive Intelligence Systems should be guided by a constitution rooted in first principles:
- Diagnose before prescribing
- Build systems over heroics
- Preserve knowledge
- Extract reusable frameworks
- Optimize long-term capability
- Challenge assumptions respectfully

This idea should inform Version 2 and any future software platform.
