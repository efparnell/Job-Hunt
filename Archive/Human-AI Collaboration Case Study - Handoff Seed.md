
# Human–AI Collaboration Case Study (Handoff Seed)

Purpose: Develop a standalone manuscript documenting this project as an example of effective human–AI collaboration.

Working goals:
- Explain how a resume review evolved into an Executive Operating Manual.
- Document the emergence of Organizational Intelligence Engineering.
- Explain the Prompt vs. Constitution distinction.
- Capture the systems engineering hierarchy applied to AI.
- Show how editorial companions preserved architectural insights.
- Describe how multiple manuscripts emerged organically from one project.
- Identify repeatable practices that others can adopt.
- Distinguish durable principles from model-specific techniques.
- Explore implications for consulting, software, and AI governance.

This document should ultimately become suitable for sharing publicly with organizations such as OpenAI or others interested in AI governance, human–AI collaboration, executive knowledge systems, and organizational intelligence.
