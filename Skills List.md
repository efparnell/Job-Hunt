## If the form asks for proficiency or years

Be honest and specific; inflated self-ratings are easy to puncture in an interview and buy nothing.

- **Expert / 10+ years:** Laboratory Operations, Analytical Chemistry, ISO/IEC 17025, Quality Management Systems, Method Validation, Team Leadership
- **Advanced / 5–10 years:** P&L Management, Budgeting, Multi-Site Operations, LIMS, Process Improvement, Employee Training & Development, Client Relationship Management
- **Intermediate:** Forecasting, Business Development, Project Management

## What to leave off

- Anything from the consulting and e-commerce work (GivMoo, marketplace operations, software architecture) — on a laboratory application it dilutes rather than adds.
- Cannabis-specific terms. The matrix belongs in the experience entries, not in a skills list that gets scanned for identity.
- Soft-skill filler: "hard worker," "team player," "detail oriented." They occupy slots and signal nothing.

## Filling ADP's Add Skill form (Skill · Date Attained · Last Used · Proficiency · Comments)

**The Skill field is a dropdown, not free text.** Type the first word and take the closest match in their list. If something isn't there, skip it rather than forcing an odd synonym — a mismatched label scores worse than an absent one.

**Do 12–18 well rather than 40 badly.** Each takes a minute, and the list is read by a person after it's scored by a system.

**Date Attained** = the year you could first do it competently, on the job. **Last Used** = leave it current for anything still true; be honest where a skill genuinely stopped. Dates must not contradict the experience entries — claiming a bench technique "last used 2026" when the experience says you left that bench in 2019 is exactly the inconsistency these forms surface.
